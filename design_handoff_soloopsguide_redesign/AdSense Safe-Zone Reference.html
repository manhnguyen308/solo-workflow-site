A reference for editorial and implementation — not a live mockup. The article reading column carries the trust of the site; ads belong in two acceptable slots only, must be visibly distinct from prose, and must never block the page from being read.

Two optional slots. One per article, max.

Placement rules

Acceptable

  • One optional mid-article slot, between two H2 sections.
  • One optional end-of-article slot, after the prose and before the related panel.
  • One optional sidebar slot in the article aside, beneath utility cards.
  • Every ad carries a visible "Advertisement" or "Sponsored" label.
  • Ad container has a visible border or distinct background so the boundary against prose is unambiguous.

Do not ship

  • Ads above the fold in the reading column.
  • Ads styled to look like editorial callouts, "editor's picks," or related links.
  • Sticky bottom ads pinned to the viewport.
  • An ad between every section. Maximum one mid-article slot per page.
  • Ads on trust pages, the homepage hero, search results above the fold, or the 404 page.
  • Pop-ups, interstitials, autoplay video ads.

Reading this diagram

  • Blue-outlined block — acceptable ad slot.
  • Red-outlined block — pattern we will not ship.
  • The two pages share the same article skeleton. The only difference is where the ads land and how they are labelled.

Why these rules

  • The reading column carries the trust of the site. Anything above the fold there must be prose.
  • Ads disguised as editorial copy break the bargain of the page and are explicitly forbidden by the content policy.
  • Sticky and interstitial formats fight the reader for control of the viewport. The site is a reading surface.
  • One mid-article slot is the practical compromise. Two slots in the body, plus one in the aside, is the ceiling — not a target.

This page is reference only. It is not embedded in any live page mockup, and it does not document the current production layout — display advertising is not enabled on SoloOpsGuide at the time of this kit. If and when ads are enabled, the cookies and analytics sections of the Privacy Policy must be re-walked alongside this diagram.
