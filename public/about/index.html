About

Solo Workflow Systems exists to help freelancers, consultants, and small solo-led service businesses build practical operating systems for client work.

This is not a generic software review site. The editorial model is workflow-first: define the client lifecycle, decide where operational truth lives, then choose the lightest stack that can run the system reliably.

Who this site is for

  • Freelancers and consultants running active client delivery and trying to reduce admin drag.
  • Solo service businesses that need clearer handoffs from intake through billing and offboarding.
  • Lean operators choosing tools under budget constraints and without dedicated ops staff.

What we publish

  • workflow anchor pages,
  • implementation guides,
  • scenario-based comparisons,
  • templates and checklists,
  • support FAQ and glossary pages.

What we avoid

  • generic “best tools” listicles with weak decision logic,
  • trend-driven tool coverage without workflow relevance,
  • filler pages that do not help a reader make or implement a decision.

How recommendations are framed

  • We prefer process clarity over app hype.
  • We state tradeoffs and recommendation boundaries explicitly.
  • We route readers to the next practical page instead of treating each article as a dead end.
