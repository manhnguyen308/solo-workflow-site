Delivery QA Checklist Before Client Handoff

A pre-handoff QA checklist to reduce revisions, prevent missing deliverables, and improve delivery confidence.

template implementation Apr 2, 2026

Use this page for

Run this checklist before sending work to clients so handoff quality is consistent across projects.

Page type

Template page with operational guidance, not general blog content.

Use this checklist before every delivery handoff, even for “small” updates. Most client frustration comes from small misses, not major failures.

Pre-handoff QA checklist

A) Scope alignment

  • Confirm deliverable matches agreed scope.
  • Confirm requested revisions are incorporated.
  • Confirm out-of-scope items are documented separately.

B) Technical/content quality

  • Validate file integrity and formatting.
  • Verify links, references, and assets.
  • Confirm naming/versioning conventions are correct.

C) Client readiness

  • Add short summary of what is included.
  • Add clear usage/implementation notes where needed.
  • Add explicit request for approval or next action.

D) Operational controls

  • Log delivery date in system of record.
  • Update milestone status.
  • Trigger next invoice or next-stage action if applicable.

Final handoff message structure

  • What is being delivered now.
  • What changed from prior draft/milestone.
  • What client should review first.
  • What response is needed and by when.

Common QA misses

  • Sending files with outdated versions.
  • Missing one dependency file or access detail.
  • No explicit next-step request.
  • Delivery logged in email but not in system of record.
