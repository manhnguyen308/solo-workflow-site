Glossary

System of Record

The single source of truth where active client status and operational decisions are maintained.

glossary informational Updated Apr 2, 2026

Use this page for

A system of record reduces handoff errors by ensuring one authoritative place for client workflow state.

Page type

Glossary page with operational guidance, not general blog content.

A system of record is the one place where the current truth about a client engagement is maintained.

For solo service businesses, this should include at least:

  • current client stage,
  • next required action,
  • current owner,
  • key dates and status.

Why it matters

If your status lives in multiple places (email, docs, chat, PM board), handoffs break and follow-ups are missed. A single system of record reduces ambiguity and rework.
