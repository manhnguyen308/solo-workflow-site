Glossary

Workflow Handoff

A workflow handoff is the transfer of responsibility, context, and required inputs from one stage to the next.

glossary informational Updated Apr 2, 2026

Use this page for

Clear handoffs reduce rework, missed details, and delivery delays in solo client operations.

Page type

Glossary page with operational guidance, not general blog content.

A workflow handoff is the point where one stage of client operations ends and the next begins.

In practical terms, a handoff is complete only when three things transfer together:

  1. required context,
  2. required assets/data,
  3. clear next owner.

If any of these are missing, the next stage starts with ambiguity.

Why this matters for solo operators

Solo businesses often have hidden handoffs (for example, from discovery notes to proposal, or from delivery to invoicing). Even when one person owns both stages, documenting the handoff reduces mistakes and context loss.

For full context, see Freelance Client Workflow System: Inquiry to Final Payment.

In practice

Use definitions to sharpen decisions

This term matters only when it improves how you design a workflow, choose a system of record, or clarify ownership. It should reduce ambiguity, not add jargon.
