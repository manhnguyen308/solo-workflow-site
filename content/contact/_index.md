Use this page for content corrections, factual updates, and partnership requests.

Current contact status:
- A public inbox is not published on the site yet.
- Until a monitored address is available, treat this page as the contact policy and routing note.
- Subject format for corrections once the inbox is published: `Correction: [Page Title]`

When reporting a correction, include:
1. page URL,
2. the specific claim,
3. the suggested correction and source/context.

## Contact publishing note

This site intentionally avoids listing an unmonitored inbox. A real public address should only be added when someone is actively reviewing corrections, reports, and partnership requests.

## Best use of this inbox

- factual corrections or broken-page reports
- workflow examples that would improve a guide
- partnership or sponsorship inquiries aligned with the site's editorial policy

For content methodology and disclosure standards, see [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).
