SoloOpsGuide does not currently publish a monitored email address, contact form, or public correction channel.

Do not send personal, contractual, billing, or other sensitive information to an address or account that merely claims to represent the site. No unlisted address or social account is an authorized contact route.

## Corrections and site issues

A direct correction request cannot currently be submitted through this site because no monitored channel is available.

The [Editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) explains how confirmed factual errors, broken routes, and unsupported claims are handled when they are found during review.

## If contact becomes available

Any future monitored method should be listed on this page and reflected in the relevant policies. Until then, no response time or review capacity is promised.
