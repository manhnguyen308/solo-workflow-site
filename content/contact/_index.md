SoloOpsGuide aims to keep public contact limited to messages that can be reviewed responsibly. This page explains what kinds of outreach are appropriate and what information is most useful.

## What this page is for

Appropriate reasons to contact SoloOpsGuide include:
- factual corrections,
- broken links or page errors,
- editorial clarifications,
- useful workflow examples that would improve a page,
- partnership or sponsorship inquiries that fit the site's scope.

## What this page is not for

This site is not set up for:
- general customer support,
- free consulting requests,
- bulk outreach,
- cold pitches unrelated to workflow, operations, or editorial partnerships.

## Best use of contact

### For corrections

Include:
1. the page URL,
2. the specific claim or section,
3. the suggested correction,
4. source, evidence, or operating context where relevant.

### For broken-page reports

Include:
1. the page URL,
2. what appears broken,
3. device or browser context if relevant.

### For partnerships

Keep it specific. The most useful messages explain:
- who you are,
- what the proposed partnership is,
- why it fits SoloOpsGuide readers,
- whether it affects editorial independence.

## Contact availability note

A public inbox is intentionally not published until it can be actively monitored. SoloOpsGuide would rather publish no public address than list one that invites legitimate correction or partnership messages into a dead inbox.

When a monitored public contact method is available, it will be added here.
