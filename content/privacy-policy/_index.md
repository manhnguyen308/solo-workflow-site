SoloOpsGuide aims to collect as little personal data as reasonably possible for a public editorial site.

## What may be collected

Depending on the tools used to operate the site, SoloOpsGuide may collect:
- basic visit analytics,
- standard server or hosting logs,
- limited technical information used for security, performance, or abuse prevention,
- information voluntarily provided through a future contact method.

## What the site is not trying to do

SoloOpsGuide is not designed to:
- sell personal data,
- require account creation to read core content,
- collect unnecessary personal information,
- use invasive tracking where simpler measurement is enough.

## Cookies and analytics

If analytics or advertising tools use cookies or similar identifiers, SoloOpsGuide aims to keep that use limited to:
- understanding site performance,
- improving content usefulness,
- supporting reasonable site operations and monetization.

If the data practices change materially, this page should be updated to reflect that change.

## Third-party services

Some site functions may rely on third-party infrastructure, such as hosting, analytics, search, or advertising tools. Those providers may process technical data as part of operating the service.

Readers should also review the relevant third-party policies where appropriate.

## Contact and data questions

Questions about privacy, corrections, or data handling should follow the contact guidance on [Contact](/contact/). If no monitored public contact method is listed there yet, it means the site has not published one yet.
