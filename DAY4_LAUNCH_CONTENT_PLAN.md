# Day 4: Launch Content Production Plan & First Publishing Roadmap

## 1) Launch content strategy

- The first batch must prove this is a workflow-first operator site, not a generic tool-list blog.
- “Enough to launch” means users can go from problem → workflow model → stack decision → implementation checklist without dead ends.
- Pre-launch priority: publish connected workflow anchors, practical guides, comparisons, and assets in each major client lifecycle stage.
- Post-launch can wait: long-tail glossary expansion, secondary comparison variants, and advanced downloadable packs.

## 2) Page role model for launch

- **Pillar page**: broad problem map; routes users to deeper pages.
- **Workflow anchor page**: end-to-end process for a real solo-operator scenario.
- **Supporting guide**: step-level implementation instructions.
- **Comparison page**: scenario-based selection decisions.
- **Monetization-supporting page**: selection intent page where transparent monetization can be added later.
- **Asset page**: checklist/template for immediate execution.
- **Glossary/support page**: term clarity and recurring blockers.

## 7) Launch readiness criteria

Minimum by type:
- Workflow pages: 2
- Practical guides/blueprints: 6
- Comparison pages: 4
- Template/checklist pages: 4
- Glossary/FAQ pages: 2

Ready-to-launch thresholds:
- At least 10 high-confidence pages (per Day 3 standards).
- Every category page has intro + at least 3 child pages.
- Homepage clearly routes by category + workflow pathway.
- Trust pages published: About, Methodology, Contact, Disclosure.
- No orphan pages.

## 8) Content production constraints

- Do not overbuild long-tail comparisons early.
- Do not publish monetization-heavy pages without workflow guidance around them.
- Easiest to get wrong: comparison pages (generic/repetitive drift).
- Perfection unnecessary: visual polish and exhaustive tool coverage.
- Quality-critical areas: workflow anchors, implementation guides, and linking structure.

## 11) Risks in launch content planning

- Too many similar pages too early (intent cannibalization).
- Monetization-heavy sequencing before trust-building pages.
- Publishing isolated pages with weak pathway design.
- Missing workflow anchor pages (site drifts to tool-first identity).
- Thin checklist pages without context/instructions.
